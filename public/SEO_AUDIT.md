# KS DIGITAL: Complete SEO Audit & Growth Recommendations
Site: ksdigital.co.in | Audit date: 7 September 2026

## 1. Executive Summary
KS Digital has a clean, small brochure-style website with five primary pages: Home, About Us, Services, Pricing, Contact Us. The biggest opportunity is a search-intent-led architecture: dedicated landing pages for each commercial service, stronger proof and topical depth, better E-E-A-T signals, and a content engine around digital marketing problems and services. Services (Performance Marketing, SEO, Social Media Marketing, Website Design, Marketing Automation, Graphic Design) are currently sections on shared pages, not dedicated indexable pages.

## 4. Critical On-Page Findings
- Home H1 is generic: "Boost Your Online Presence with Digital Marketing Services." Does not differentiate KS Digital or target a customer.
- Broad phrases like "data-driven digital marketing" repeat without evidence or quantified outcomes.
- Services page lists six services but no dedicated service URLs in primary nav.
- About page has placeholder content: "John Doe", "Add team member description here". Remove immediately.
- About page repeats generic template-driven FAQ content.
- Contact page contains "No content is added yet". Remove.
- Spelling issues: "Content Planing", "Campaign Repporting". Fix site-wide.
- About lists a senior SEO advisor with 14+ years experience: a strong E-E-A-T asset. Build a detailed expert profile linked to relevant SEO content.
- Services page mixes pricing with service descriptions. Separate broad service education from package/pricing intent.

## 8. Home Page Recommendations
- Replace generic H1 with a specific proposition, e.g. "Digital Marketing That Turns Traffic Into Measurable Growth."
- Add supporting line specifying core services and target customer segment.
- Two primary CTAs: "Book a Strategy Call" and "View Our Services."
- Add proof above the fold: clients served, years of experience, spend managed, leads generated, organic growth, or other VERIFIED metrics.
- Case-study strip with real before/after results. Do not use unverified numbers.
- Service grid where each service links to its dedicated landing page.
- Industries served only if genuine specialization.
- Testimonials with client name, company, role where permission exists.
- "Why KS Digital" section based on actual differentiators.
- Keep FAQs but rewrite around real pre-sales questions; add FAQPage schema only when visible FAQ content qualifies.

Each service page contains: definition, business problems solved, process, deliverables, ideal customer, proof/case study, FAQs, pricing guidance where appropriate, related services, strong CTA, internal links.

## 11. Pricing Page Recommendations

- Add "best for" descriptions.

## 12. Contact Page Recommendations
- Remove "No content is added yet."
- Add business address if publicly applicable, phone, email, working hours.
- Short statement on what happens after form submission.
- Direct call/WhatsApp CTA.
- Organization/LocalBusiness schema only with accurate, verifiable details.
- Google Map only if a genuine customer-facing location exists.

## 13. Technical SEO Checklist
- HTTPS: maintain and force one canonical HTTPS version.
- XML sitemap: confirm /sitemap.xml exists, is clean, submitted in Search Console.
- Robots.txt: confirm crawl directives and sitemap declaration.
- Canonical tags: one self-referencing canonical per indexable page.
- Indexability: check noindex, X-Robots-Tag, canonical conflicts.
- 404/redirects: eliminate broken internal links and unnecessary chains.
- Core Web Vitals: test mobile and desktop.
- Mobile UX: tap targets, font sizes, CLS.
- Images: WebP/AVIF, compression, explicit dimensions, descriptive alt text.
- JavaScript: critical content and links available to crawlers without client-side dependency.
- Breadcrumbs: visible on deeper service/content pages.
- Schema: only relevant types with matching visible content.
- Open Graph: title, description, image, URL.
- Twitter/X cards: appropriate card metadata.

## 16. E-E-A-T & Trust
- Real, detailed Team/expert page instead of thin bios.
- Author boxes and profile pages connected to content.
- Original case studies: starting point, work performed, timeframe, result, methodology.
- First-party evidence: screenshots, charts, campaign examples, anonymized dashboards.
- Certifications and platform expertise only where valid.
- Legal/trust pages: Privacy Policy, Terms, Cookie Policy, Refund/Cancellation if applicable.
- Consistent business name, phone, email, website, social profiles across the web.

## 17. Local SEO
If targeting a specific city/region: optimize Google Business Profile; collect genuine reviews; build consistent NAP citations; create location pages only for locations actually served with unique useful content; build local partnerships/PR rather than low-quality directories.

## 18. Backlink / Authority Strategy
- Digital PR, expert commentary, original research, founder/expert interviews.
- Link to service pages via relevant articles, not only home page.
- Linkable assets: SEO checklists, marketing calculators, benchmark reports, data studies.
- Competitor backlink analysis for relevant publications.
- Avoid bulk guest-post packages, PBNs, irrelevant directories.

## 19. AI Search / GEO
- Authoritative pages that answer commercial questions clearly and directly.
- Concise definitions, comparison tables, process explanations, original evidence.
- Entity consistency around KS Digital, founder, experts, services, client outcomes.
- Answer: who is this for, how much does it cost, how does it work, what are the alternatives, what results can I expect.
- Strengthen third-party mentions via legitimate PR, expert contributions, reviews.
- Do not create large volumes of generic AI-generated pages.

## 20. 90-Day Roadmap
- Days 1-15: fix placeholders, spelling, titles/descriptions, H1s, canonicals, sitemap/robots, analytics/GSC validation, internal links, contact/trust pages.
- Days 16-30: launch SEO, Performance Marketing, Social Media, Web Design pages; improve Home/About/Pricing/Contact.
- Days 31-60: publish 8-12 high-intent articles, 2-4 case studies, expert profiles, schema, stronger internal linking.
- Days 61-90: digital PR/link acquisition, refresh content from Search Console data, industry/location pages where justified.

## 21. Measurement Framework
- Search Console: clicks, impressions, CTR, average position, indexed pages, query/page performance.
- GA4: organic sessions, engaged sessions, lead submissions, phone clicks, WhatsApp clicks, qualified conversions.
- Business: MQLs, SQLs, CAC, revenue influenced by organic.
- Page groups: service pages, location pages, blog, case studies, brand pages.
- Monthly reporting: new rankings, ranking movement, non-brand organic clicks, leads, conversion rate.

## 23. Audit Limitation
Based on publicly accessible pages and rendered content. Private Search Console data, GA4, server logs, crawl directives, real Core Web Vitals, backlink profile, rankings and full HTML source were not validated.

## 24. Verdict
The site is closer to a company brochure than a search-led acquisition website. The biggest growth lever is moving from five broad pages to a structured topical architecture with dedicated service pages, proof-led content, expert entities, case studies and high-intent informational content.
